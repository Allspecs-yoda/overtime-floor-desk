# California 9×5 vs FLSA 40

A $22/hour tech-support shift, 9 hours × 5 days = 45 hours.

```bash
python3 desk/quote.py --quote CA --hourly 22 --hours 45 --days 5 --daily
python3 desk/quote.py --quote TX --hourly 22 --hours 45 --days 5
```

CA Labor Code §510: 1.5× after 8/day (5 hours) plus 40-hour week. TX is FLSA 40 only (5 hours of 1.5×). Same clock, different premium. Floor on DOL 2026-07-01: CA $16.90, TX $7.25.

Not wage-and-hour advice.
